# Uniform birth representation and conditional mean audit

Review by the integrating agent, independently of the author of these modules.
Source: unchanged `attachement-uniforme-1-final.tex`, lines 1402–1429.
Scope: A40 and the exact birth-probability inputs of A41. The asymptotic
normalization and moment estimates of A41–A42 are reviewed separately.

The source uses independent unmasked Bernoulli variables of parameter
$1/S_i$, independent of the actual uniform vertex's reverse birth time.
The attachment indicator is applied inside the sum, and the birth step is
included. `UniformBirth.reciprocalParameter` uses $S_{i+1}$ on a zero-based
`Fin n` index. `truncatedSum` selects indices less than the birth time, so
it includes exactly paper times $1,\ldots,B$. The reciprocal parameters
are justified by `PositiveThrough`; no representation or independence
premise is added.

`actualBirthHeightLaw_eq_auxiliary` proves the joint birth/height law from
the actual reverse forest kernel and conditional uniform vertex sampling.
Its proof uses the already proved individual-vertex reverse depth law,
interchanges the genuine forest and uniform-label kernels, and identifies
the masked independent array. The auxiliary space is the explicit product
of the actual birth law and the unmasked Bernoulli product. Separate
coordinate-law and independence theorems establish both kinds of source
independence.

`randomMean_condExp` is conditional expectation with respect to the
sigma-algebra generated by the birth coordinate. Its Fubini/set-integral
proof checks the defining conditional-expectation identity and
integrability; it does not assume a conditional mean formula.
`randomMean_eq_heightScale_birth`, `randomMean_bounds`, and
`randomMean_ge_half` prove respectively $M_n=h_{B_n}^+$, the source bounds,
and $M_n\ge1/2$ almost surely for every positive horizon. The zero-horizon
sum and mean are zero. Thus future divisions by $M_n$ have a proved
positive-row justification.

`birthLaw_lt` is the exact integer strict CDF, with denominator
$n+1+S_n$ and numerator $m+1-S_m$. `auxiliary_sublinear_birth_tendsto`
derives negligible early-birth probability from this CDF; the real
sublogarithmic cutoff is converted using its ceiling.

Source correction S21 is checked: $h_n^+-M_n$ is a sum over $B<i\le n$.
`heightScale_sub_truncatedMean` uses `Finset.Ioc B n`.
`printed_inclusive_birth_tail_false` proves that the printed inclusive
equality is false already for the all-positive one-step schedule. This
does not invalidate use of the larger inclusive sum as an upper bound.
